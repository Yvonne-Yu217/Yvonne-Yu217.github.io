Forecast design

Forecast regressions use earlier observations

Given the daily sentiment series, I replicated expanding-window OLS against a historical-mean benchmark.

Scaling, OLS fitting, and the benchmark use observations through t−1.

Forecast evidence

ChiNext has the strongest full-period forecast improvement

Out-of-sample R² compares squared prediction errors with the historical-mean forecast.

The 0.293% is forecast-error improvement, not an investment return.

Inference and stability
