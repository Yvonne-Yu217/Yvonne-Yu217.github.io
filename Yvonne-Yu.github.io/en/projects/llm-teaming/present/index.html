Small-LLM Teaming · Paper presentation 1 / 8

Research question

When and where does model collaboration help?

Different tasks offer different places to collaborate: final answers on short contexts, or retrieved evidence from longer passages.

Task structure determines whether there is useful intermediate evidence for a team to select.

Small-LLM Teaming · Paper presentation 2 / 8

Study design

Four benchmarks contrast two reasoning settings

The evaluation contrasts short-context reasoning with long-passage natural-language inference; each comparison uses the same validation questions.

ConTRoL provides an evidence-selection stage that the shorter tasks largely lack.

Small-LLM Teaming · Paper presentation 3 / 8

Short-context evidence

Answer agreement does not improve these tasks

Voting, debate, and judging underperform Qwen2.5-7B direct answering on all three short-context benchmarks.

Correlated errors and unreliable confidence are diagnostic explanations, not separately identified causes.

Small-LLM Teaming · Paper presentation 4 / 8

Move to long passages

Pooling alone stays close to single-model retrieval

The study next moves to ConTRoL: three models pool premise sentences, and one model answers from the union.

Against RQA, pooling changes accuracy by +0.1 and −0.6 points here, motivating a closer look at evidence selection.

Small-LLM Teaming · Paper presentation 5 / 8

Evidence architecture

Verify shared evidence before one model answers

The verification experiment switches to a Qwen3-8B answerer; retrieval and checking are shared before one of two answering paths runs.

Retained-evidence answering and direct fallback are exclusive routes; both use the original input and the same answerer.

Small-LLM Teaming · Paper presentation 6 / 8

Main comparison

The complete F1 pipeline gains 6.2 percentage points

With Qwen3-8B fixed as answerer, F1 reaches 71.7% accuracy versus 65.5% for single-model retrieval.

The gain compares complete F1, including fallback, with RQA; it does not isolate the verifier’s effect.

Small-LLM Teaming · Paper presentation 7 / 8

Validation and interpretation

Faithful evidence may still be irrelevant

F1 accepts fewer candidates and falls back more often; the character-level audit separately checks whether retrieved evidence is supported by the premise.

Evidence acceptance, fallback, and textual support measure different properties; this audit does not evaluate final-answer hallucination.

Small-LLM Teaming · Paper presentation 8 / 8

Conclusion

Match the collaboration mechanism to the task

The experiments distinguish sharing answers, pooling evidence, and verifying evidence; their results come from different task and model configurations.

The evidence favors testing collaboration at the evidence stage; generalization and deployment efficiency remain open.
